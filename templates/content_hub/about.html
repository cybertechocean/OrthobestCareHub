Who We Are

Dedicated to Your Recovery, Comfort & Mobility

Orthobest Care Hub (formerly Orthobest Care Hab / Rehab) is a premier supplier of certified orthopedic supports, mobility aids, physiotherapy gear, and clinical equipment in Nairobi, Kenya.

Our Story & Mission

Founded with a commitment to making high-grade rehabilitation equipment easily accessible to Kenyan patients and healthcare institutions, Orthobest Care Hub operates as a trusted partner for post-surgery recovery, chronic pain management, and mobility enhancement.

From our clinical showroom located on Mfangano Street, Travis Building, 3rd Floor (Room C37, Wing C) in Nairobi CBD, we deliver certified products across all 47 counties in Kenya with personalized sizing support via direct phone and WhatsApp consultations.

Our Mission

To provide patients, caregivers, and medical facilities across East Africa with durable, verified orthopedic and mobility solutions that restore independence and quality of life.

Our Vision

To become Kenya's most trusted, technologically seamless health-commerce platform for specialized physical therapy and orthopedic equipment.

Quality & Standards

We source exclusively from certified medical manufacturers, ensuring that every hinge, strap, wheel, and mattress meets anatomical safety guidelines.
